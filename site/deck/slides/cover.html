KaleidoPay

Receive what you want.
Pay with what you have.

A plugin for wallets: one QR for the receiver, every route for the payer.

KaleidoPay is a plugin for wallets that improves the experience on both sides of a payment. The receiver shares one QR that says how they want to be paid, in order of priority. The payer pays with whatever they hold, directly or through swap providers. Walter and Mo, bitcoin++ Berlin 2026.
