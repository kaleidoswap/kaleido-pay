KaleidoPay

Pay with what you have.
Receive what you want.

One reusable QR. The receiver says what they accept. The payer's wallet finds a route.

KaleidoPay is a payment flow inside Rate, KaleidoSwap's mobile wallet. The receiver says which layers they accept, and in what order. The payer pays with whatever they hold, and the wallet finds a route between the two. Walter and Mo, bitcoin++ Berlin 2026.
