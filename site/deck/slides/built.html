Left: what existed before the hackathon. Rate, KaleidoSwap's mobile wallet, already had Spark, Arkade, RGB, a Lightning NWC client and a QR scanner. wallet-engine is the multi-protocol wallet library behind it. The SSPS spec drafts were private, and already had the idea of rails in a BOLT12 offer. ldk-test-env was a regtest fixture with LDK nodes and an NWC bridge. Right: what we built from 1 to 3 October. Bark in Rate and wallet-engine (the BarkReactNativeAdapter, by Mo), and Bark paying BOLT12 offers (wallet-engine PR #119). The LDK forks (rust-lightning, ldk-node, ldk-server) issue offers that carry ssps_rails, including Bark and Arkade address entries and amountless reusable offers. New NWC methods for reusable offers with ordered rails and receipt lookup: kaleidopay_make_offer and kaleidopay_list_offer_payments. universal-code: the rails record codec, BIP321 plus offer, network detection and a route planner. swap-market: a client for Electrum's swap providers over Nostr (discovery, quotes, reverse swaps, claim, recovery), plus our own providers on signet and Mutinynet. KaleidoPay in Rate: the receiver's "Reusable payment QR" with ordered preferences, and the payer review with all routes, provider comparison, Bark direct, Lightning and on-chain routes, and recovery. SSPS published under CC0, with a Bark rail and review fixes. And the demo videos.
