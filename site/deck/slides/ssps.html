The standard

SSPS: Swap Service Provider Specification

An open standard, live today, announced next week. Built on the BOLTs and on Electrum's Lightning ↔ on-chain swap providers on Nostr.

ONE PROTOCOL

Many providers

Providers announce on Nostr which layers they serve. Wallets ask several at once.

EXAMPLE

On-chain wallet pays Lightning

Pick the cheapest of several providers, swap inside the payment.

THE PROMISE

Pay any address

As long as at least one provider implements a route, the wallet can pay it.

SSPS, the Swap Service Provider Specification, is an open standard we published today and will announce next week. It builds on the BOLTs and on the chain-Lightning swap providers Electrum runs over Nostr. A wallet can query several providers at the same time and choose the best, for example to pay a Lightning invoice from an on-chain-only wallet. KaleidoPay's promise: pay any address out there, as long as one provider implements the route. github.com/kaleidoswap/ssps
