How the receiver side works

BOLT12, with the receiver's priorities inside

The receiver's Rate connects to their own LDK node over Nostr Wallet Connect and passes the ranked layers and addresses. The node issues one reusable BOLT12 offer with that list in an extra record.

WHAT WE CHANGED

A patched LDK

LDK authenticates every record of its offers, so the list must go in at creation. We patched rust-lightning, ldk-node and ldk-server.

COMPATIBLE

Still a BOLT12 offer

Wallets that ignore the record pay it over Lightning as usual.

TRADE-OFF

Address reuse

Ark addresses in the offer are static. Next: an Ark protocol for universal QR codes without reuse.

Implementation: Rate talks to an LDK node over NWC, sends the ordered rails and addresses, and the node issues a BOLT12 offer carrying them in the ssps_rails record. We had to patch LDK because its offer HMAC covers every record. The honest trade-off: Ark addresses in a reusable offer mean address reuse; the next step is an Ark protocol for universal QR codes.
