In Rate: Receive, then "Reusable payment QR". The receiver connects their own Lightning node, an LDK node, over Nostr Wallet Connect. They pick the layers they accept (Bark, which is Second's Ark, Arkade, Lightning, on-chain) and put them in order. Rate saves the preference for future offers. The node then issues one reusable BOLT12 offer with that order inside. Lightning is always accepted, because the offer itself is a Lightning offer.
