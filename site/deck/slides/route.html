Direct routes: the payer's wallet already speaks a layer the receiver accepts. Bark pays the receiver's Bark address on the same Bark server with no swap, pays the BOLT12 offer over Lightning, or sends on-chain itself through Second's server. Swap routes: Electrum's swap providers advertise on Nostr, and we talk to them with Electrum's own swap protocol over NIP-04 encrypted messages. Bark pays a hold invoice plus a prepayment over Lightning, the provider locks coins on-chain, and the wallet claims them straight to the receiver's address. On mainnet, four providers quoted about 1,050 to 1,070 sats in fees on 30,000 sats.
