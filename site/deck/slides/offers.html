BIP321: bitcoin:<address>?lno=<offer> · forks on feat/offer-ssps-rails

The order travels in an experimental offer record, ssps_rails, TLV type 1000000385, defined in section 5.3 of the open SSPS spec. Bark and Arkade entries carry the receiver's address and the Ark server's key. Lightning is always accepted. A BIP321 link, bitcoin:address?lno=offer, carries the on-chain address next to the offer. The detail people ask about: LDK authenticates its own offers with an HMAC over all records, so a record cannot be appended later; the node must add it when it creates the offer. We patched rust-lightning, ldk-node and ldk-server (branch feat/offer-ssps-rails) so the node issues offers with the record, including amountless reusable ones. A wallet that does not know the record still sees and pays a normal BOLT12 offer.
