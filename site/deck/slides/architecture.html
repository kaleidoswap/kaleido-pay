The top row happens once. The receiver's Rate talks to their own LDK node over Nostr Wallet Connect, and the patched node issues one reusable BOLT12 offer with the ordered rails inside. The bottom row happens on every payment. The payer's Rate scans the offer, quotes the direct routes (Bark, Lightning, on-chain) and the Electrum swap providers it finds on Nostr. The payer picks one, and the receiver is paid on a layer they accept.
