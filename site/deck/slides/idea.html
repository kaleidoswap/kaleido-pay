The idea

One code says what you accept.
The payer pays with what they have.

RECEIVER

Says what they accept

Picks layers and puts them in order. Gets one reusable QR.

THE CODE

A BOLT12 offer with the order inside

Any BOLT12 wallet can still pay it.

PAYER'S WALLET

Finds a route

Pays directly, or through a swap provider. Every fee shown first.

The receiver publishes one code: a BOLT12 offer that carries the layers they accept, in order. A wallet that does not understand the extra record pays it as a normal offer. A wallet that does reads the order, quotes every route it can take, and lets the payer pick.
