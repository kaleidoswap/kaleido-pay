The idea

A wallet plugin for both sides of a payment

RECEIVER

One QR, your priorities

Choose every way you accept money and rank them. Share a single reusable code.

PAYER

Pay with what you have

Scan any code. Pay directly if you share a layer, otherwise through the best swap provider.

OPEN STANDARD

SSPS

Swap providers speak one open protocol, found on Nostr. Any address is payable if one provider supports it.

KaleidoPay sits inside a wallet. For the receiver it builds one QR with ranked options. For the payer it turns any scanned code into a list of routes. The glue between them is SSPS, an open standard for swap providers.
