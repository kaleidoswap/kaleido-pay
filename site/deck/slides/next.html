What becomes possible. One QR for any wallet: Bark, Arkade, Lightning, on-chain, and later Liquid and RGB-Lightning assets. The receiver states a preference, the payer's wallet finds a route. Swap providers compete on every payment, in an open market on Nostr: Electrum's providers today, SSPS makers next. Negotiated per-payment locks, ssps_lock, so a payer can pay on-chain or on Ark without Lightning in between. Next steps: a bLIP for the offer record, contributions to LDK, Bark BOLT12 support in wallet-engine (the PR is open), and a mainnet receiver node over NWC with real payments end to end.
