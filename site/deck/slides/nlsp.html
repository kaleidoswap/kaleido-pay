Liquid, Arkade, RGB on Lightning. Arkade, Spark or a node as payer. Every provider that can route quotes.

KaleidoPay is not tied to Bark. The payer's wallet only needs to read the receiver's rails and pay Lightning; Bark is the first wallet we wired. Everything else comes from Nostr Lightning Swap Providers: services that announce on Nostr which rails they deliver to, quote a swap from Lightning, and lock the receiver's asset on the receiver's rail for the same payment hash as the payer's hold invoice, so both settle together or not at all. Electrum's swap providers are the first ones, Lightning to on-chain bitcoin, live on mainnet. The same pattern gives a receiver USDT on Liquid: they list the USDt rail, the payer pays sats over Lightning, a provider such as a KaleidoSwap maker locks USDt on Liquid, and the claim pays the receiver's address. The payer never touches USDT. Same for L-BTC, Arkade, RGB assets on Lightning, and other payer wallets.
